ZenTask is a task and project manager that works entirely on your device — no account required, no data leaves your phone unless you choose to turn on cloud sync.

TASKS & PROJECTS
Rich tasks with priority, due dates, reminders, subtasks, tags, and custom labels. Organize them into projects and track completion at a glance. Pin your most important tasks, save your favorite filters, and set up recurring tasks for things you do every day, week, or month.

AI PLANNER
Describe a goal in plain language and get back a structured plan of tasks — powered by Claude, when you provide your own API key. Works entirely without one too; every AI feature degrades gracefully to a clear message rather than a broken screen.

FOCUS & HABITS
A built-in Pomodoro-style focus timer with session statistics, daily habit tracking with streaks, measurable goals with progress bars, and achievements that unlock as you build momentum.

CALENDAR & ANALYTICS
See task due dates, reminders, and events together in one calendar view. Track your completion rate, streaks, and productivity trends over time.

PRIVACY & SECURITY, BY DEFAULT
Your data stays on your device unless you explicitly connect a cloud account. Turn on biometric app lock and AES-256 encryption of your local data any time from Settings. Delete everything with one tap, whenever you want.

OPTIONAL CLOUD SYNC
Connect an account to sync your tasks and projects across devices — entirely optional, and the app is fully functional without it.

No ads. No trackers. No account required to get started.
